# 👋 Hello, visitor!

<div style="display: flex; align-items: flex-start; gap: 2.5rem;">
  <div style="flex: 1;">
    My name is Klest Dedja. I am a Data Scientist with a background in Applied Mathematics and Machine Learning, combining theoretical rigor with hands-on software engineering. My early experience focused on mathematics and financial engineering, which later evolved into a PhD centered on <strong>Explainable AI</strong> for <strong>Survival Analysis</strong> (time-to-event modeling under censoring and partial information), with applications in healthcare domains such as Multiple Sclerosis progression and kidney function estimation.

    During this period, I developed strong expertise in <strong>Random Forests</strong> and tree-based ensembles, particularly for  tabular data. By leveraging the internal structure of these models, I built <a href="https://github.com/klestdedja/bellatrex"><strong>Bellatrex</strong></a>, an open-source Python toolbox for extracting faithful, locally accurate rule-based explanations from ensemble models. This work required not only algorithmic design, but puhed me into learning about software packaging, testing, and appreciating reproducible workflows.

    Beyond interpretability, I explored <strong>Active Learning</strong> strategies tailored to data settings with partial information (again: survival analysis).

    More recently, my focus has expanded toward production-oriented AI systems. I am actively developing skills in <strong>DevOps and MLOps</strong>, including CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, containerization, and reproducible deployments.
    At the same time, I am exploring <strong>Agentic AI systems</strong>, learning how to set-up and manage multi-agent and tool-augmented architectures. I am jut getting started, but I already love to build software this way!
